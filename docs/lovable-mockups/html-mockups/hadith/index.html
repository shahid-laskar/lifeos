Hadith 1

Sahih
إِنَّمَا الْأَعْمَالُ بِالنِّيَّاتِ، وَإِنَّمَا لِكُلِّ امْرِئٍ مَا نَوَى
“Actions are judged only by intentions, and every person will have only what they intended. So whoever emigrated for the sake of Allah and His Messenger, his emigration was for Allah and His Messenger. And whoever emigrated for worldly gain or to marry a woman, his emigration was for that which he emigrated.”
Narrated by ‘Umar ibn al-Khattab (may Allah be pleased with him). Chain: al-Humaydi ‘Abdullah ibn az-Zubayr → Sufyan → Yahya ibn Sa‘id al-Ansari → Muhammad ibn Ibrahim at-Taymi → ‘Alqama ibn Waqqas al-Laythi.
